SAND MIXER

Sand mixing machine is a mechanical device used for mixing sand, binders, and water to produce molds and cores for casting metal. It is an essential tool in the foundry industry, where metal casting is a critical process.

Sand mixers are strong machines for heavy-duty applications and offered 12TPH to 70 TPH as a single machine.

Classic Sand Master

Advanced sand testing and moisture control.

View Details

CLASSIC SAND MASTER

The Classic Sand Master measures compatibility and sand strength from a 50 mm test sand sample extracted from the mixer. Water is added to the mix based on the compatibility measurements. From the sand strength measurements, the bentonite requirements are calculated and can be adjusted in small increments in following batches towards a target sand strength.

As options, the Classic Sand Master can include measurements of return sand temperature and moisture before the mixer. This is particularly relevant for low moisture contents, or for return moisture contents and sand temperatures with large variations.

Water is added in two stages : Pre-water in the beginning of the mixing cycle Trim-water after measurement of compatibility. If the moisture sensor option is included, then up to 100% pre-water can be added.

Automatic water addition via compatibility : The Classic Sand Master automatically takes and samples from the mixer, measures compatibility and sand strength and determines the necessary amount of water.

The Classic Sand Master automatically adapts itself to the sand

Nobake Process

Complete chemical binder casting systems.

View Details

NOBAKE PROCESS

ROLL OVER MACHINE

FLOOD COATING

CONTINUOUS MIXTURE

It is a casting process that involves the use of chemical binders to bond the molding sand. Sand is conveyed to the mold fill station in preparation for filling of the mold. A mixer is used to blend the sand with the chemical binder and catalyst. As the sand exits the mixer, the binder begins the chemical process of hardening. This method of mold filling can be used for each half of the mold (cope and drag). Each mold half is then compacted to form a strong and dense mold. A rollover is then used to remove the mold half from the pattern box. After the sand has set, a mold wash may be applied. Cores, if required, are set into the drag and the cope is closed over the cores to complete the mold. A series of mold handling cars and conveyors move the mold into position for pouring. Once poured, the mold is allowed to cool before shake-out. The shake-out process involves breaking the molded sand away from the casting. The casting then proceeds to a casting finishing area for riser removal, casting finishing and finalization. The broken pieces of molded sand are further broken down until the sand is returned to grain size. The sand can now be either reclaimed for reuse in the casting process or removed for disposal. Thermal reclamation is the most efficient, complete method of Nobake sand reclamation.
